Hardware Price Tracker

Raspberry Pi boards, DDR4/DDR5 memory kits and 1 TB SSDs, priced weekly in Canadian dollars and recorded in a local SQLite database. A scheduled job on my own hardware collects the listings and redraws the graph below; the raw data never leaves the machine. Part of pehkonen.dev.

What is tracked

  • Raspberry Pi 5 / Pi 4 — 4GB & 8GB at PiShop.ca, CanaKit, Seeed Studio, and Adafruit (USD listings converted to CAD).
  • Memory kits at Canada Computers — DDR4-3200 16GB, DDR5-6000 32GB and 64GB, and DDR5-6000 32GB SODIMM.
  • 1 TB SSDs — M.2 NVMe and 2.5-inch SATA drives for a laptop upgrade at Canada Computers, plus the Crucial and SK hynix models it does not stock at Newegg.ca.
Collected weekly on self-hosted hardware · Static HTML & CSS · No cookies
